# MASTER FRONTEND BUILD PROMPT — BHOOMISETU

You are acting as a **principal frontend architect + senior product designer + interaction designer + frontend performance engineer** building a production-quality government intelligence platform.

Build the frontend for **BhoomiSetu — Predictive Land Acquisition Delay Intelligence Platform**.

The product should look and behave like a serious **enterprise/government decision-intelligence system**, not a generic hackathon dashboard.

The visual/product quality bar should feel comparable to modern products from **Google, Meta, Palantir, Stripe, Linear and Vercel**, while remaining appropriate for an Indian government/public-sector environment.

---

# 1. PRODUCT PURPOSE

BhoomiSetu helps government authorities detect, understand and proactively mitigate **land acquisition delays**.

The frontend must communicate:

**DATA → RISK → EXPLANATION → INTERVENTION → ACTION → MONITORING**

The interface should allow an officer/administrator to:

* monitor statewide acquisition health
* identify high-risk districts/projects
* understand why a project is at risk
* inspect project lifecycle stages
* monitor compensation and financial bottlenecks
* identify legal/documentation/ownership risks
* compare districts and states
* generate policy/action briefings
* assign interventions
* track intervention status
* inspect geographic intelligence
* make decisions quickly from dense information

Do NOT make the UI look like a collection of unrelated cards.

The entire application must feel like **one coherent intelligence operating system**.
